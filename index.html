Meet Coach Mr. Krishna

At White Birdie Badminton Academy, coaching is more than just hitting shuttles — it’s about nurturing athletic agility, mental focus, and genuine passion for the sport. Under Coach Krishna’s mentorship, players of all skill levels receive dedicated, personalized attention.

🎯 Personalized Skill Plans

Customized stroke correction, racquet grip mechanics, and shadow drills tailored to each player's learning pace.
